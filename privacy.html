Privacy Policy

AbayBids · Abay Technical & Trading s.c · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the AbayBids app (the “app”) handles, in particular the Google user data it uses when the company connects a Google Drive backup. The app is an internal tool of Abay Technical & Trading s.c (“we”). Its home page describes what it does.

1. Information the app handles

  • Business records entered by our staff: tenders, bids, clients, contracts, deadlines, notes and the documents attached to them. They are kept in the company's own cloud database and on the company's office computer.
  • Staff account details: name, e-mail address and role, used to sign in and to decide what each person may do.

2. Google user data

If, and only if, a company administrator chooses to connect Google Drive, the app asks the Google account owner for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.

  • What the app can access: only the folder AbayBids Backup and the files that the app itself created in it. It cannot see, read, change or delete any other file in the Google Drive, and it does not read the account's e-mail, contacts, calendar or photos.
  • What it is used for: (a) copying the company's bid documents into that folder as a backup, and (b) reading those backup copies back when a staff member asks to open a document while the office computer is off, or when the office computer has to be restored.
  • Sign-in credential: Google gives the app a renewable sign-in token (a “refresh token”). It is stored on the company's office computer. So that staff devices can open backed-up documents when the office computer is off, the token is also stored in the company's private cloud database, where it can be read only by signed-in staff accounts of the company and changed only by an administrator.
  • Basic account information: the app reads the e-mail address of the connected Google account, only to show which account holds the backup.

3. How we use and share information

  • We use information only to run the app for the company's own tender work and to provide the backup described above.
  • We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising or for building user profiles, and do not transfer it to anyone else, except to the storage providers the company itself chose to run the app (for example the company's cloud database and, if enabled, Backblaze B2 as a second backup) or where the law requires it.
  • No person reads the content of backed-up documents through Google Drive other than the company's authorised staff opening them in the app.
  • The app does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

AbayBids’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage, security and retention

Documents in Google Drive stay there until the company removes them. The app never deletes company documents from the backup folder. Data in transit is protected with HTTPS. Access to the app requires a company account, and what each person may see depends on their role.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Disconnect: an administrator can press Disconnect in the app’s settings. The app then asks Google to cancel the sign-in, forgets the token and removes the copy shared with staff devices.
  • Revoke from Google: the Google account owner can remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the backup: the owner can delete the AbayBids Backup folder in Google Drive at any time.
  • To ask about or request deletion of any other information we hold, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its date.
