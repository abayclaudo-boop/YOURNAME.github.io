AbayBids

Smart Tender System · Abay Technical & Trading s.c

AbayBids is the internal tender management system of Abay Technical & Trading s.c. Our staff use it on Windows computers and Android phones to follow tenders and bids, keep track of deadlines and contracts, and keep every bid document in one organised place.

What the app does

  • Records tenders and bids, their status (pending, participated, won, lost) and their deadlines, with reminders.
  • Manages contracts, their start and end dates and their renewal reminders.
  • Stores bid documents (for example tender documents, contracts and certificates) on the company's main office computer, organised by company, tender and category.
  • Shows dashboards and reports for management.

Optional backup to the company's Google Drive

An administrator of Abay Technical & Trading s.c can connect the company's own Google account so that the documents saved by the app are also copied to a folder named AbayBids Backup in that Google Drive. If the office computer is switched off, staff can then still open a document from this backup.

The app asks for only one Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it the app can see and manage only the folder and files it created itself. It cannot see or change any other file in the Google Drive.

Details of how Google user data is handled are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

AbayBids is a private tool for the employees of Abay Technical & Trading s.c. Accounts are created by the company's administrator; it is not offered to the public.
